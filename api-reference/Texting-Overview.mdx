## How it works

The Texting endpoints let you send one-off SMS messages — either from templates we configure for your account, or as free-form text you write yourself. Your account is identified by your `collectwise_key` API key, sent in the header on every request.

<Note>
  Texting must be enabled for your organization before these endpoints will respond. If you receive a `403` on every call, contact us to have your organization enabled.
</Note>

A typical integration is:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover templates">
    Call [List SMS Templates](/api-reference/endpoint/Text-List-Templates) to see the templates available to your account and the placeholder variables each one expects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Preview segments">
    Call [Calculate SMS Segments](/api-reference/endpoint/Text-Segments) to see how many SMS segments a message or rendered template will use, before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Call [Send a Text](/api-reference/endpoint/Text-Send) with a `destination`, a `debtorId`, and either a `templateId` plus a `variables` object filling in every placeholder, or a free-form `message`. A `200` means the message was accepted and queued, and returns a `messageId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check status">
    Poll [Get Text Status](/api-reference/endpoint/Text-Status) with the `messageId`. Delivery reports arrive asynchronously, so check after a short delay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive replies">
    Register an [Inbound Reply Webhook](/api-reference/Inbound-Reply-Webhook) to have every reply pushed to you in real time, or poll [List Text Messages](/api-reference/endpoint/Text-Messages) for the thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two ways to send

Every send is one of two mutually exclusive mechanisms. Send exactly one of `templateId` or `message` — a request with both, or with neither, is rejected with `400`.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Template" icon="file-lines">
    Supply a `templateId` and a `variables` object. We render the template's `{placeholder}` tokens and send the result. The copy is pre-approved with the carrier.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free-form" icon="pen-to-square">
    Supply a `message`. We send exactly what you wrote, up to 1600 characters, with nothing substituted and nothing appended.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Free-form texts

Free-form sending is enabled per organization and is **off by default** — sending a `message` without it returns `403 Free-form texts are not enabled for this organization`. Contact us to have it turned on.

<Warning>
  Free-form text bypasses the carrier's pre-approved template copy, so compliance wording is entirely your responsibility. Include your opt-out language (for example, "Reply STOP to opt out") and any agency identification your messages require — we do not append anything to a free-form message.
</Warning>

Apart from the body, a free-form send behaves exactly like a templated one: same response, same `messageId`, same delivery status, same replies. In [List Text Messages](/api-reference/endpoint/Text-Messages), free-form sends have a `templateId` of `null`.

## Templates and variables

Each template has a `templateId` and a `message` body containing `{placeholder}` tokens. The `placeholders` array on a template lists exactly which keys you must supply in `variables` when sending. If any placeholder is left unresolved, the send fails with `400` rather than texting a literal `{placeholder}` to the recipient.

Placeholders use single curly braces and are matched exactly first, then normalized (case-insensitive, ignoring `#` and spaces), so `{FirstName}`, `{#FileNumber}`, and `{Variable Name}` all work.

## Segments and encoding

SMS messages are billed and split into segments by character count and encoding, matching the [Twilio segment calculator](https://twiliodeved.github.io/message-segment-calculator/):

- **GSM-7**: 160 characters in a single segment, 153 per segment when the message spans multiple segments.
- **UCS-2**: 70 characters single, 67 per segment when multi-part. Any character outside the GSM-7 set (emoji, smart quotes, most CJK) forces the entire message to UCS-2.
- The extended GSM-7 characters `{ } [ ] ~ \ | ^ €` cost two units each.

## Delivery status

A message moves through `Queued` → `Sent` → `Delivered`, or ends in `Rejected` / `Failed` / `Undelivered`. For failed messages, `failureClassification` indicates whether a retry is worthwhile:

| Classification | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `FATAL` | Will never deliver as-is (e.g. invalid number, blocked, flagged as spam). Do not retry. |
| `NON_FATAL` | Transient (e.g. absent subscriber, temporary carrier failure). `retryRecommended` is `true`. |
| `null` | The carrier reason was not recognized. |

## Receiving replies

Consumers can reply to any text you send. There are two ways to get those replies, and they can be used together:

- **[Inbound Reply Webhook](/api-reference/Inbound-Reply-Webhook)** — we push every reply to an HTTPS endpoint you own, signed so you can verify it came from us. Send us your endpoint URL to have it configured.
- **[List Text Messages](/api-reference/endpoint/Text-Messages)** — read the thread on demand, in both directions. Use this if you would rather poll than run an endpoint, or with the `since` parameter to backfill after a webhook outage.

Each reply carries an `inReplyTo` field pointing back at the `messageId` of the send it answers, so you can tie a response to the message that prompted it. It is `null` when the reply cannot be attributed to one of your sends — a consumer texting in cold, or a conversation that has gone idle for more than 7 days.

## Rate limits

- **Send**: 10 requests per minute per organization.
- **Reads** (templates, segments, status, messages): 60 requests per minute per organization.

Exceeding a limit returns `429` with a `Retry-After` header.
